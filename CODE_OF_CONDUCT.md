# Code of Conduct

We want TEST Squadron Discord Bot to be welcoming, respectful, and useful for
everyone. Contributors and maintainers are expected to participate
professionally and constructively.

Expected behavior includes respecting differing viewpoints, giving and
accepting constructive feedback, protecting private information, and keeping
technical discussions clear and kind. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
personal attacks, doxxing, trolling, and deliberate publication of sensitive
data are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md).
Security vulnerabilities must follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not a
public issue. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take
other appropriate action. Reports will be handled confidentially where
practical.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
